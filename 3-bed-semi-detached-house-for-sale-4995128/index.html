About the property

Description property

GWD Estates are proud to bring to the market this wonderful three-bedroom semi detached family home (with the added advantage of a detached garage to the rear), ideally located just a short walk from Killester dart station. No. 282 is bright and spacious throughout providing the perfect blank canvas for purchasers to re-style, modernise and re-decorate the property according to their own taste, if so desired.

Positioned in the heart of Dublin 5 close to Artaine Castle shopping centre and within easy reach of Beaumont Hospital as well as Clontarf Golf club and the Howth Road. There are numerous primary & secondary schools close by as well a large selection of shops, cafes and bars. The bus stop is literally outside your door bringing you into the city centre quickly. The M1 & M50 are easily accessed from this property making its location on the Malahide Road a big feature for anyone looking to live in this area.

Accommodation: Entrance porch leading to large hallway with understairs storage. Lounge with wooden floors and feature fireplace. Dining room with carpet and feature fireplace. Kitchen with floor units with ceramic floor and wall tiles. Small outside back porch. Upstairs there are three large bedrooms with wooden floors and built in wardrobes and a family bathroom.

The property has Gas fire central heating and double Glazed Pvc windows and is alarmed.

There is a detached garage to the rear from a private laneway and the front garden has ample room for 4 family cars.
